# Customer Privacy Notice

This notice is the fictional customer-facing summary for the Helios Ledger bookkeeping subscription. It is demo text.

## Clause 2.1 Who the notice names

The notice names Helios Ledger Ltd, a fictional company, as the organisation that runs the subscription. The Dublin address in the notice is also fictional and is not a real office.

## Clause 2.2 How a customer complains

A customer may write to the privacy inbox about the subscription and may also complain to a supervisory authority. The notice gives a postal route and does not publish a direct line for an individual employee.

## Clause 2.3 What the subscription uses

Account details are used to run the bookkeeping subscription, to bill the subscription, and to keep the service available. Marketing is covered by a separate clause in the marketing policy.
