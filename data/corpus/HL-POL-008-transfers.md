# International Transfers

This policy is the fictional rule for moving customer information outside the EEA. It is demo text.

## Clause 3.2 Transfer tool

Customer personal data is not transferred outside the EEA unless the transfer tool is standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision recorded in the transfer register. A transfer with no recorded tool is stopped.

## Clause 4.1 Register contents

The transfer register entry names the importer, the tool, and the date of the last review. The entry is updated when the importer changes.

## Clause 5.3 Staff travel

A business trip with a laptop is not recorded as a transfer under this clause when the laptop stays in the staff member's control and no copy is left with a third party.
