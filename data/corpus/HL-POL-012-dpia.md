# DPIA Procedure

This procedure says when the fictional company writes a data protection impact assessment. It is demo text.

## Clause 2.1 Credit feature

A DPIA is required before launching a feature that scores the creditworthiness of a sole trader. The assessment is opened before any design that uses that score is built.

## Clause 3.1 Sign-off

The DPIA is signed by the Data Protection Lead before launch. Launch includes a beta that real customers can open.

## Clause 4.4 Small visual changes

A DPIA is not required for a change that only updates the colour of a button. The product owner records that the change is visual only.
