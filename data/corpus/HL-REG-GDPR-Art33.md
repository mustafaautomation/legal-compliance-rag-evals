# GDPR Article 33 excerpt

The passages below are short excerpts for this demo. Read the official EUR-Lex text before relying on any wording.

## Article 33 Notification excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 33 states that the controller shall without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after having become aware of it, notify the personal data breach to the supervisory authority, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

## Article 33 Content excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 33 states that the notification shall at least describe the nature of the personal data breach, where possible the categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned, and the name and contact details of the data protection officer or other contact point.
