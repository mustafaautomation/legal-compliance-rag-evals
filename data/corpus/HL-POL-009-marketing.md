# Marketing and Cookies

This policy is the fictional marketing and cookie rule for Helios Ledger Ltd. It is demo text.

## Clause 2.1 Prospects

Marketing email to prospects is sent only with prior consent. A purchased list is not used to start that email.

## Clause 2.2 Existing customers

Existing customers may receive product-service emails about similar bookkeeping features, and every message carries an unsubscribe link. The link is tested each quarter.

## Clause 6.1 Cookies

Non-essential cookies stay off until the user opts in. Essential cookies that keep the session open are described in the cookie panel and do not wait for that opt-in.
