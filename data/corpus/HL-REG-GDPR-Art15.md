# GDPR Article 15 excerpt

The passages below are short excerpts for this demo. Read the official EUR-Lex text before relying on any wording.

## Article 15 Access excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 15 states that the data subject shall have the right to obtain from the controller confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning him or her are being processed, and, where that is the case, access to the personal data.

## Article 15 Copy excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 15 states that the controller shall provide a copy of the personal data undergoing processing. The excerpt does not set the company procedure for checking identity.
