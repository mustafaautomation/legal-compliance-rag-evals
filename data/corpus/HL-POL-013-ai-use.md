# AI Use Policy

This policy is the fictional rule for staff use of drafting tools. It is demo text.

## Clause 2.2 Public chatbots

Staff must not paste customer personal data into a public AI chatbot. A prompt that includes a customer name, an invoice, or a ledger row is not pasted into a public tool.

## Clause 3.1 Approved drafting tool

The approved tool for internal drafting is Helios Draft, which is hosted in the EEA and covered by vendor register entry VD-014. Other drafting tools are not approved for customer text.

## Clause 4.2 Human check

Output from Helios Draft is checked by a person before it is sent to a customer. The checker is named on the outgoing message ticket.
