# Customer DPA Roles

This note explains the fictional split of roles in the Helios Ledger customer data processing agreement. It is demo text.

## Clause 2.2 Role split

In the customer DPA, Helios acts as processor for bookkeeping records the customer uploads, and as controller for its own account, billing, and security logs. The role is stated on the first page of the DPA.

## Clause 3.1 Customer responsibility

The customer remains responsible for the lawfulness of the ledger contents it uploads. Helios does not decide why a customer collected those contents.

## Clause 4.3 Where suppliers are listed

Suppliers that handle uploaded records are listed in the subprocessor schedule. This note does not repeat the supplier names.
