# GDPR Article 17 excerpt

The passages below are short excerpts for this demo. Read the official EUR-Lex text before relying on any wording.

## Article 17 Erasure excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 17 states that the data subject shall have the right to obtain from the controller the erasure of personal data concerning him or her without undue delay, and that the controller shall have the obligation to erase personal data without undue delay where a listed ground applies.

## Article 17 Legal claims excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 17 states that the erasure duty does not apply to the extent that processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
