# GDPR Article 6 excerpt

The passages below are short excerpts for this demo. Read the official EUR-Lex text before relying on any wording.

## Article 6 Lawfulness excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 6 states that processing shall be lawful only if and to the extent that at least one of the following applies. Point (a) says the data subject has given consent to the processing of his or her personal data for one or more specific purposes.

## Article 6 Contract excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 6 states that point (b) covers processing necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract.
