# Acceptable Use

This policy is the fictional rule for how staff handle working files. It is demo text.

## Clause 3.3 Exports

Staff must not store customer exports on personal laptops. Exports stay in the approved vault.

## Clause 4.1 Passwords

Passwords are not shared between staff and are not written in chat. A shared login for a production system is replaced with named accounts.

## Clause 5.2 Lost devices

A lost laptop or phone is reported to the security queue the same day. The report names the device and the last known location.
