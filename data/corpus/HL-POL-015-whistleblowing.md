# Whistleblowing

This policy is the fictional path for a report about how information is handled. It is demo text.

## Clause 2.1 Report about the lead

A whistleblowing report that concerns the Data Protection Lead goes to the independent board member named in this clause. The board member is a role in this fictional policy and is not a real person.

## Clause 3.1 Other reports

A whistleblowing report about other staff goes to the Data Protection Lead. The lead records the report and keeps the reporter's name limited to the people who need it.

## Clause 4.2 Retaliation

Retaliation against a reporter is a disciplinary matter. A manager does not decide the outcome of a report that names that manager.
