# GDPR Article 32 excerpt

The passages below are short excerpts for this demo. Read the official EUR-Lex text before relying on any wording.

## Article 32 Security excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 32 states that the controller and the processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

## Article 32 Measures excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 32 lists examples that include pseudonymisation and encryption, the ability to ensure ongoing confidentiality, and a process for regularly testing the measures. The excerpt does not name a product setting.
