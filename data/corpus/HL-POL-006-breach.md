# Personal Data Breach Procedure

This procedure is the fictional incident path for Helios Ledger Ltd. It is demo text and does not describe a real incident.

## Clause 2.1 Escalation

A suspected personal data breach is escalated to the Data Protection Lead within 4 hours of discovery. The person who notices the incident writes the time of discovery on the incident ticket.

## Clause 3.3 Notification decision

The Data Protection Lead decides whether to notify the supervisory authority. Any notification under this procedure is made within 72 hours of Helios becoming aware of the breach.

## Clause 4.1 Where details are written

Incident notes stay in the security queue. Staff do not post incident details in a public chat room or in a personal mailbox.
