# Data Subject Request Procedure

This procedure is how fictional Helios Ledger staff handle a request from a person about their information. It is demo text.

## Clause 2.4 Time to respond

Data subject access requests are acknowledged within 5 working days and answered within one month. The clock starts on the working day the request arrives in the request inbox.

## Clause 4.1 Checking identity

Identity is verified with two pieces of information already held on the account before records are released. Staff do not ask the requester to send a copy of a passport to a personal inbox.

## Clause 5.2 Repeated requests

A request that repeats a request already answered can be marked excessive and paused until the requester narrows the scope. The pause is recorded on the request ticket.
