# Data Retention Schedule

This schedule is the fictional retention rule for Helios Ledger Ltd. Periods below are demo figures, not a statement of what a real firm must do.

## Clause 3.1 Customer invoices

Customer invoices and account records are kept for six years after the contract ends. The records team then deletes or anonymises those invoices within 90 days.

## Clause 4.2 Recruitment records

Recruitment records for unsuccessful applicants are kept for 12 months. The talent team deletes the application file at the end of that period.

## Clause 5.1 Server access logs

Server access logs are kept for 180 days. The security team deletes the log store after that period unless a notified incident investigation is still open.

## Clause 6.4 Supplier campaign file

A supplier campaign file is kept for 24 months after the last supplier campaign. This clause is about suppliers and not about customer invoices.
