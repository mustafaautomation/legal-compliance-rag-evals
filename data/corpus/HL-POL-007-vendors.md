# Vendor and Processor Due Diligence

This policy is how the fictional company takes on a supplier that will handle customer information. It is demo text.

## Clause 2.3 Before a processor starts

A processor is not engaged until a written Article 28 contract is signed and the security review is marked pass in the vendor register. A verbal promise from a salesperson is not enough.

## Clause 5.1 No sale to brokers

Helios does not sell customer personal data to data brokers. A proposal to do so is rejected and recorded by the procurement owner.

## Clause 6.2 Register review

The vendor register is reviewed every January by the procurement owner. The review checks that each open engagement still has a signed contract and a passing security review.
