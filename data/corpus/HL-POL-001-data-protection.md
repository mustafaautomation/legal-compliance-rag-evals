# Data Protection Policy

This policy is the internal rulebook for staff who handle information at Helios Ledger Ltd. The company, the address, and the people named in the library are fictional.

## Clause 1.1 Who the policy covers

The rulebook applies to employees and contractors who can open the bookkeeping product or the staff tools. It does not apply to a customer's own end users except where a later clause says so.

## Clause 1.2 Who owns the library

The Data Protection Lead owns this library and reviews the text once each calendar year. Department heads send corrections to that lead rather than editing a clause in place.

## Clause 1.3 Questions from staff

A staff question is answered from the indexed clauses. The library does not appoint the reader as counsel, and it does not predict a regulator's decision.
