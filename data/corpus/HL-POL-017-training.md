# Data Protection Training

This policy is the fictional training rule for Helios Ledger staff. It is demo text.

## Clause 1.1 When training is due

All staff complete data protection training within 14 days of joining and a refresher each calendar year. The people system assigns the course on the joining date.

## Clause 2.2 What the course covers

The training covers how to recognise a suspected incident and how to file a person request. It does not give a script for a court hearing.

## Clause 3.1 Missed refresher

A person who misses the refresher is blocked from production access until the course is done. The block is lifted the day the course is recorded as complete.
