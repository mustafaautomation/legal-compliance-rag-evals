# Employee Privacy Notice

This notice describes how the fictional company handles staff files. It is demo text.

## Clause 3.1 Payroll

Payroll details are used to pay salaries under the employment agreement and to meet a legal duty to deduct tax. The notice does not state a corporate tax rate.

## Clause 3.2 Workplace records

Badge records may be used to look into a workplace safety incident. They are not used to score the performance of a salesperson.

## Clause 3.3 Where periods are listed

How long an employee file is kept is listed in the retention schedule. This notice does not repeat those periods.
