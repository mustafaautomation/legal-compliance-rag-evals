# GDPR Article 5 excerpt

The passages below are short excerpts for this demo. Read the official EUR-Lex text before relying on any wording.

## Article 5 Principles excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 5 states that personal data shall be processed lawfully, fairly and in a transparent manner, collected for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes, and kept limited to what is necessary. The excerpt does not reproduce the whole of Article 5(1).

## Article 5 Accountability excerpt

This excerpt of GDPR Article 5 states that the controller shall be responsible for, and be able to demonstrate compliance with, paragraph 1 ('accountability').
